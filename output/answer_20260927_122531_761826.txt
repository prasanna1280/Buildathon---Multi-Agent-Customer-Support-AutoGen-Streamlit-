Query:
How do I reset my password?

Assistant Answer:
To reset your password, you typically need to follow these steps:

1. Go to the login page of the website or app.
2. Click on the “Forgot Password?” link or similar option.
3. Enter your email address or username associated with your account.
4. Check your email for a password reset link or code.
5. Follow the instructions in the email to create a new password.

If you're having trouble, refer to the help section of the specific service for more detailed guidance.
