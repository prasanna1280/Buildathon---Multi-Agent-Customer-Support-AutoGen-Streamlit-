Query:
How do i reset my password in gmail?

Assistant Answer:
To reset your Gmail password, follow these steps:

1. Go to the Gmail sign-in page.
2. Click on "Forgot password?" under the password field.
3. Enter the email address for the account you want to recover and click "Next."
4. Follow the prompts to verify your identity. This may include answering security questions or receiving a verification code via your recovery email or phone number.
5. Once your identity is verified, you'll be able to create a new password.
6. Enter and confirm your new password, then save the changes.

After successfully resetting your password, make sure to update it in any applications or devices that use your Gmail account.
