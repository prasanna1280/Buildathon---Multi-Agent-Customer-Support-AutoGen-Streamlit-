Query:
How do I reset my password?

Assistant Answer:
To reset your password, follow these general steps, though the exact process may vary depending on the platform or service:

1. **Go to the Login Page:** Visit the login page of the website or service.

2. **Click on 'Forgot Password' or Similar Link:** Look for a link that says "Forgot Password?" or "Reset Password."

3. **Enter Your Email or Username:** Provide the email address or username associated with your account.

4. **Check Your Email:** Look for an email from the service with instructions on how to reset your password. Make sure to check your spam/junk folder if you don't see it in your inbox.

5. **Follow the Instructions:** Click on the link provided in the email and follow the instructions to create a new password.

6. **Log In with Your New Password:** Once your password is reset, you can log in with your new password.

If you encounter any issues, check the service's help or support section for specific instructions.

Web Search Answer:
To reset your password, the steps vary slightly depending on the service you're using. Here are some specific links and general instructions for popular services:

1. **Google Account:**
   - Go to the [Google Account recovery page](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/41078?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid).
   - Click on "Forgot password?" and follow the prompts to reset it.

2. **Apple Account:**
   - Open your device's Settings app.
   - Tap your name, then go to "Sign-In & Security."
   - Tap "Change Password," enter your device passcode, and follow the onscreen instructions. For detailed steps, check [Apple's support page](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102656).

3. **Microsoft Account:**
   - Visit the [Microsoft account password reset page](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/accounts-billing/manage/reset-a-forgotten-microsoft-account-password).
   - Click on "Forgot password?" and follow the instructions provided there.

If you're trying to reset a password for a different service, the typical steps usually involve visiting the login page, finding a "Forgot Password" link, and then following the email verification process to create a new password.
